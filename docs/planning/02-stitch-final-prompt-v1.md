# Stitch Prompt — refashionlab v1

Design a mobile-first editorial content website called refashionlab. It helps complete beginners learn sewing, repair everyday clothes, and turn existing garments or fabric into useful projects. The audience is English-speaking global beginners. The tone is calm, practical, encouraging, and non-judgmental. The site should feel like a well-used sewing notebook: warm, tactile, clear, and quietly confident.

Use a warm cream paper background, deep green ink, muted sage surfaces, terracotta/rust accents, fine rules, generous whitespace, and restrained rounded corners. Use a consistent text wordmark “refashionlab” in the header and footer. Do not invent an icon, mascot, monogram, animal mark, or separate logo symbol.

Create these page types, each in desktop and narrow mobile layouts around 360–390px:

1. Homepage: compact header; hero with the headline “Make useful things from what you already have.”; four beginner pathways; three Section cards; featured real guide titles; a practical material discovery block; an editorial promise; and a footer linking About, Privacy Policy, Terms, and User Agreement.
2. Section landing: intro, parent context, category cards, and featured/latest guide cards. Use the three Sections Sewing Foundations, Repair & Refashion, and Everyday Sewing Projects.
3. Category hub: topic introduction, parent Section link, five post cards, and a clear next step.
4. Post detail: breadcrumb, category context, long title, direct-answer opening, Published date state, optional Updated state, long-form reading column, h2/h3 structure, materials and preparation, numbered steps, troubleshooting, FAQ when genuine, related links, and footer.
5. Legal/trust page: shared header and footer, readable long-form typography, headings, paragraphs, lists or links where needed, and no marketing clutter.

Use real content examples: “How to Fix a Hole in Jeans by Hand: A Beginner Repair Plan”, “How to Read a Sewing Pattern for Beginners Without Feeling Overwhelmed”, “10 Easy T-Shirt Upcycles for Beginners Using Clothes You Already Own”, and “Best Beginner Sewing Supplies: A Buy-Less, Make-More Checklist”. Keep text natural and source-supported; do not use lorem ipsum, sample SaaS copy, or invented claims.

Mobile is the primary composition. Keep touch targets comfortable, avoid horizontal overflow, stack cards cleanly, let long titles wrap, keep article line length readable, and use minimal motion with reduced-motion support. Do not add accounts, comments, contact forms, subscriptions, payments, downloads, dashboards, or unrelated features.

Return a multi-page Stitch candidate package with desktop and mobile screenshots for Homepage, Section, Category, Post, and Legal page types, plus the exported HTML/CSS/assets and a short DESIGN.md describing the final tokens and page structure. This is a candidate for human review; do not label it approved.
