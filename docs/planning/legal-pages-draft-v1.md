## About

Route: `/about/`

SEO title: `About refashionlab | Beginner Sewing and Refashion Guides`

SEO description: `Learn what refashionlab covers, who it helps, and how our beginner sewing, repair, and upcycling guides are organized.`

refashionlab is a practical guide for people who are new to sewing, clothing repair, and upcycling. The site helps readers understand basic techniques and complete useful projects with clothing, fabric, and tools they may already have.

We cover sewing foundations, sewing tools, sewing-machine basics, hand sewing, visible mending, denim refashion, old T-shirt upcycling, before-and-after projects, fabric bags, and home sewing.

Our articles are written for clarity and everyday use. They organize information around real beginner questions, explain steps in plain language, and connect readers to related projects when useful. Content may be updated as the site's scope and information change.

The content is general DIY and sewing information. It is not professional tailoring instruction, product certification, emergency guidance, or a guarantee that a project will work with every material, tool, or skill level. Readers should use appropriate care with needles, scissors, irons, sewing machines, and other tools.

Our reader promise is to make sewing feel more approachable, reduce unnecessary jargon, separate practical guidance from opinion, and keep projects readable on mobile.

Official contact information will be published when contact channels are enabled for this site.
